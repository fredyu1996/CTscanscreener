# Pattern notes

Cross-case knowledge that accumulates as the base grows. Each entry is a descriptive
pattern and the discriminators that separate it from its look-alikes — not a diagnostic
rule. Add to this file whenever a case teaches something reusable, and link the case IDs
that demonstrate it.

Entries start empty of case links; they fill in as the base grows.

## Chest

### Solid pulmonary nodule
Round soft-tissue density within aerated lung. What matters for the record: size
(longest axis), margins (smooth / lobulated / spiculated), presence of calcification and
its pattern, and location by lobe.
**Discriminators** — central/laminated/popcorn calcification and stability over 2 years
favour benign; spiculation, upper-lobe location, and growth favour concern.
**Cases:** _(none yet)_

### Ground-glass opacity
Hazy increased attenuation that does not obscure underlying vessels and bronchi.
**Discriminators** — consolidation obscures vessels, ground-glass does not. Peripheral
and basal distribution vs perihilar changes the differential entirely.
**Cases:** _(none yet)_

### Consolidation
Homogeneous opacification obscuring vessel margins, often with air bronchograms.
**Discriminators** — lobar and sharply marginated at a fissure vs patchy and multifocal.
**Cases:** _(none yet)_

### Pleural effusion
Dependent fluid-density collection layering posteriorly on supine axial imaging.
**Discriminators** — HU near water = simple; higher HU, loculation, or enhancing pleura
suggests exudate, blood, or empyema.
**Cases:** _(none yet)_

## Abdomen

### Simple hepatic cyst
Well-defined, water-density (≤ 20 HU), imperceptible wall, no enhancement.
**Discriminators** — any septation, mural nodule, wall thickening, or HU above ~20
moves it out of "simple".
**Cases:** _(none yet)_

### Haemangioma
Discontinuous peripheral nodular enhancement with centripetal fill-in on delayed phase.
**Discriminators** — the fill-in pattern is the point, and needs multiphase imaging. On
a single phase it is not distinguishable from other hypervascular lesions.
**Cases:** _(none yet)_

### Renal cyst vs solid renal mass
Cyst: water density, thin wall, no enhancement. Solid mass: soft-tissue density,
enhances.
**Discriminators** — Bosniak categorisation depends on septations, wall thickness,
calcification, and enhancement; it needs pre- and post-contrast imaging.
**Cases:** _(none yet)_

### Free intraperitoneal gas
Gas-density collections outside bowel lumen, non-dependent — anterior to the liver on
supine imaging.
**Discriminators** — distinguish from bowel loops by non-anatomic distribution;
best seen on lung window even on an abdominal study. Critical if genuine.
**Cases:** _(none yet)_

### Bowel obstruction
Dilated proximal loops with a calibre change and collapsed distal bowel.
**Discriminators** — small bowel > 3 cm, colon > 6 cm, caecum > 9 cm. The transition
point is the finding worth localising.
**Cases:** _(none yet)_

## Head

### Acute intracranial haemorrhage
Hyperdense (50–100 HU) on non-contrast, conforming to a compartment — extradural
(biconvex, crosses dural attachments, stops at sutures), subdural (crescentic, crosses
sutures), subarachnoid (fills cisterns and sulci), or intraparenchymal.
**Discriminators** — shape and compartment, plus mass effect and midline shift. Critical.
**Cases:** _(none yet)_

### Early ischaemic change
Loss of grey–white differentiation, insular ribbon sign, sulcal effacement, hyperdense
vessel sign.
**Discriminators** — subtle in the first hours and window-dependent; a stroke window
makes it visible where standard brain window does not.
**Cases:** _(none yet)_

## How to add an entry

Give the pattern a heading, describe what is actually seen, list the discriminators that
separate it from its mimics, and append the case IDs demonstrating it. Keep it
descriptive — this file records what things look like, not what to do about them.
